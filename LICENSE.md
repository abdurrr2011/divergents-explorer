No general open-source licence has been selected for the project code. Do not infer a redistribution grant from repository access.

Third-party photographs retain their individual licences and credits in `src/campus-photos.mjs` and `docs/PHOTO-CREDITS.md`. Four institution records use three source photographs with unconfirmed reuse terms; obtain clearance or replace them before wider distribution.

Adapted UNESCO-UIS taxonomy labels retain the attribution and CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO terms recorded in `docs/TAXONOMY.md`. Third-party dependency licences remain with their authors. This file does not relicense third-party material or the source institutions' content.
