## Next research batches

1. Transport and logistics (one verified example): verify accessible college and university routes, including entry after Grade 9 where published.
2. Teaching and languages: add representative language, translation and teacher-training programmes.
3. Health: seek sourced nursing, pharmacy and other health routes; distinguish professional registration and diploma recognition.
4. Nature: seek agriculture and veterinary routes rather than implying current conservation examples cover them.
5. Creative fields and services: seek music, film, journalism, sport and hospitality examples.

Each addition needs an official programme page, applicant-specific admission evidence, fee scope, funding eligibility, independent cycles, translated plain names and sourced campus media. Publish verified records only; examples above are research targets, not claims of available programmes.

A teacher/counsellor should choose priorities and review admissions claims. Coverage growth should add actual programmes, not merely more filter labels.

Run `npm run coverage` to refresh the detailed region/topic report. Data Science currently has 12 options at 11 institutions. The 24-option expansion reused existing institutions; total institutions remain 33. Named majors and concentrations are labelled within their parent degrees. No additional universities were invented to inflate counts.
