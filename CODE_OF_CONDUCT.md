# Community expectations

Treat students, teachers and contributors with respect. Discuss the work, explain disagreements with evidence, and welcome people learning to contribute.

Do not post harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, spam, personal student records or private application documents. Give factual, constructive feedback and respect photographer/source attribution.

The maintainer may moderate disruptive issues, comments and contributions. Use a private maintainer channel for sensitive reports; do not expose personal information in a public issue. Security reports follow SECURITY.md. No response-time or round-the-clock moderation commitment is implied.
